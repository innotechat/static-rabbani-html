Privacy Policy

Rabbani Tower (Venus Grandeur) · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this website or contact the Rabbani Tower project team.

Current enquiry form: The website form prepares an email using your device's email application. It does not submit the form to a server-side project database.

1. Information you provide

If you contact the team by email, telephone or another channel, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, requirements and enquiry details. Avoid sending sensitive personal information unless necessary.

2. Use of information

Information provided directly may be used to respond to enquiries, share project information, arrange callbacks or meetings and maintain relevant communication records.

3. Sharing

Communications may be processed by the email, telephone or other service you choose. Information may be shared with authorised representatives assisting with your enquiry or where required by law.

4. Third-party resources

This website loads third-party resources such as web fonts, styling and icon libraries. Those providers may receive technical request information. Any analytics or tracking tools enabled later should be disclosed in an updated policy.

5. Security and retention

Reasonable care should be taken when handling enquiries. Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary or as required by law. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may contact the project team to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you provided, subject to applicable law and retention requirements.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, communication practices or applicable requirements.
