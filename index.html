01 / PRIVATE RESIDENCES

Apartment Comforts

  • Premium flooring, contemporary lighting and coordinated interior finishes
  • Modular kitchen provision, chimney/exhaust provision and practical storage
  • Bedroom wardrobe and air-conditioning provisions
  • Bathrooms with quality sanitaryware, CP fittings, hot-and-cold water and anti-skid flooring
  • Concealed electrical wiring, modular switches and appropriate circuit protection
  • High-speed internet readiness, intercom and secure entrance provisions
  • Weather-resistant windows with suitable acoustic performance

Furnished-apartment package, appliances, loose furniture and housekeeping equipment to be specified separately.

02 / ARRIVAL & SHARED SPACES

Lobby & Common Areas

  • Designed entrance lobby and reception / concierge provision
  • Visitor registration and delivery coordination
  • Passenger lifts and service-lift provision as per traffic planning
  • Intercom and controlled access at designated entry points
  • CCTV coverage of designated common areas
  • Accessible routes, step-free access and suitable common facilities
03 / WELLNESS & RECREATION

Lifestyle Amenities

  • Gymnasium with cardio and strength-training zones
  • Swimming pool and hydro-therapy facilities as planned
  • Sauna and steam wellness spaces
  • Yoga, meditation and multipurpose activity spaces
  • Indoor games, billiards and entertainment spaces
  • Children’s activity / play area
  • Landscaped podium, terrace garden and outdoor seating
04 / WORK & HOSPITALITY

Business & Guest Experience

  • Business lounge with work desks and connectivity
  • Meeting / conference facilities
  • Reception and guest assistance
  • Multipurpose gathering and event space
  • Café / pantry or food-service provision, subject to operating model
  • Guest check-in and service coordination where serviced-apartment operations apply
05 / FIRE & LIFE SAFETY

Safety Systems

  • Fire detection, alarm and manual call-point systems
  • Sprinklers, hydrants, hose reels and fire-water systems as required by approved design
  • Protected escape routes, fire-rated doors and illuminated exit signage
  • Emergency lighting, smoke management and designated emergency power
  • Fire stopping, compartmentation and fire-service access provisions
  • Inspection, testing, maintenance records and emergency procedures
06 / STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING

Structural Resilience

  • Geotechnical investigation and foundation recommendations
  • Structural design for applicable seismic, wind and imposed loads
  • RCC design and ductile detailing as required by applicable standards
  • Engineering checks for building configuration, parking levels and structural irregularities
  • Material testing, construction quality checks and structural-stage inspections
  • Professional design documentation and applicable safety certification
07 / BUILDING SERVICES

Power, Water & Environment

  • Designed electrical distribution, metering, earthing and protection
  • Backup power for designated essential services
  • Water storage, pumping, filtration and plumbing systems
  • Hot-water provision and efficient building services
  • Sewage treatment / approved sewer connection as applicable
  • Rainwater harvesting, waste segregation and responsible disposal
  • Solar PV and energy-efficient lighting / equipment where provided
  • Telecom, fibre and lightning-protection provisions as designed
08 / ARRIVAL & MOBILITY

Parking & Access Management

  • Designated resident and visitor parking as per approved plans
  • Marked parking bays and organised vehicle circulation
  • Controlled vehicle entry and exit
  • Pedestrian movement and emergency access provisions
  • Parking ventilation, drainage and CCTV where applicable
  • EV charging provision subject to electrical capacity and approved design
09 / DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Security & Smart Building

  • Security desk and monitored common-area surveillance
  • RFID / smart access control at designated points
  • Video door-phone provision where specified
  • Visitor verification and secure delivery handling
  • Central monitoring of designated building systems
  • Digital maintenance requests and resident communication options
10 / PROPERTY OPERATIONS

Serviced-Apartment Management

  • Reception, guest check-in and assistance
  • Housekeeping, linen and laundry coordination
  • Maintenance desk and complaint resolution
  • Common-area cleaning and pest-control arrangements
  • Waste collection and service coordination
  • Booking, billing and guest-management systems where operated as serviced apartments
  • Preventive maintenance for lifts, pumps, generators and safety systems
11 / OUTDOOR LIVING

Terrace & Landscaped Areas

  • Landscaped podium and terrace seating zones
  • Shaded relaxation and outdoor gathering spaces
  • Walking, light-exercise and quiet wellness areas
  • Safe access, lighting, drainage and waterproofing provisions
  • Structural assessment for planters, water features and occupied terraces
12 / QUALITY & HANDOVER

Construction & Compliance

  • Specified concrete, reinforcement and material quality controls
  • Waterproofing for roofs, terraces, wet areas and basements
  • Durable façade, windows, doors, flooring and interior finishes
  • Electrical, plumbing and equipment testing
  • Applicable sanctioned plans, inspections, approvals and completion documentation
  • Operation manuals, warranties and maintenance handover records
